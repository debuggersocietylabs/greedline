Good questions.

How do I play?

Start with $100. Tap the investment amount to choose how much to put into a trade, then tap Invest. Every move, including the first, either pays the exact offered gain or crashes the whole position. Each successful hold increases the next gain and the next crash risk, up to 82%. Sell to secure your profit, or Hold for the displayed gain and chance of a rise. The secured total is cash before outstanding debt; the main screen shows net worth. There is no fixed number of moves or rounds.

What happens in a crash?

The invested position is lost unless you have protection. Cash you kept outside the trade remains available. If a closed trade leaves you with no cash, choose to review a $100 loan or start again with $100. There is no automatic refill. Your personal best stays when you start over.

How do loans and repayments work?

Tap Debt on the main screen. You can request $100 up to three times, then must repay all debt, including interest, to unlock three more loans. Partial repayments do not restore your allowance. You can borrow and repay between moves, even during a live trade.

Each settled move adds 2% interest, rounded up to a cent. No interest is charged for waiting, selling, borrowing, or repayment. Debt of $400 or more ends the run through default. The popup shows your next interest charge; an imminent default warning appears before a move that would cross the limit. Borrowing does not increase net worth.

How does Stop loss work?

Tap Stop loss on the main screen to activate it for $35 of available game cash. It recovers 80% of the original investment on one crash. You may activate it during a trade between moves. It stays armed through successful sales until consumed. Unsold profit is lost and debt remains payable. Cash outside a position always stays yours.

How do challenges work?

Tap the trophy and choose a challenge between trades. Triple banked net worth in nine trades for $50, reach five times in seven trades for $150, or ten times in five trades for $400. Your target is based on net worth when starting, with a minimum baseline of $100. One challenge can be active at a time, with one attempt at each per run.

Only selling to reach the target counts. Debt is deducted and the reward is then credited automatically. Borrowing and unrealized gains cannot complete a challenge. Each sale or crash uses one trade; holding within the same position does not. Restarting resets challenge attempts.

How do I restart?

Tap the restart arrow beside Settings and confirm. This resets cash to $100 and clears your current position, debt, Stop loss, challenges, and run purchases. Your personal best and settings stay.

Why does the ticker dip during a winning move?

The short rallies and pullbacks make the ticker feel alive. They are visual fluctuations within a move. The settled outcome is the exact displayed gain or a complete crash, with the odds shown before your decision. Its outcome is fixed when you tap Invest or Hold; closing the app does not reroll it.

What do streaks do?

Consecutive profitable trades add 8% to the streak component of the reward formula, up to five trades. Every offered gain already includes the streak bonus and the increasing reward for holding longer.

Where is my progress saved?

Your run, debt, pending move, personal best, and preferences are saved on this iPhone. Updating to build 3 keeps your progress and unfinished outcomes. Old outstanding loans keep their original default threshold until repaid, with further borrowing locked meanwhile. Old insurance becomes 80% Stop loss and refunds the $55 price difference once. Saved desk and chart upgrades are retained. They persist across launches. There is no account or developer-operated cloud sync. We cannot recover progress that was never sent to us.

Why can’t I hear the game?

Greedline respects Silent Mode. Check Silent Mode, media volume, and Sound in the in-game settings. Haptics require supported hardware and can be turned off separately. Reduce effects and iOS Reduce Motion shorten ticker animations.

Is this real investing?

No. Greedline is a fictional game, not an investment service or a simulation of real market behavior. There are no real stocks, real-money trades, ads, or in-app payments.

Which devices are supported?

Greedline is made for iPhone with iOS 17 or later. The app is configured as an iPhone app, with a portrait layout.
