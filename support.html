Good questions.

How do I play?

Start with $100. Tap the investment amount to choose how much to put into a trade, then tap Invest. Every move, including the first, either pays the exact offered gain or crashes the whole position. Each successful hold increases the next gain and the next crash risk, up to 82%. Sell to secure your profit, or Hold for the displayed gain and chance of a rise. The secured total is cash before outstanding debt; Wallet shows net worth. There is no fixed number of moves or rounds.

What happens in a crash?

The invested position is lost unless you have protection. Cash you kept outside the trade remains available. If a closed trade leaves you with no cash, choose to review a $100 loan or start again with $100. There is no automatic refill. Your personal best stays when you start over.

How do loans and repayments work?

Open Wallet to request $100 at a time. Review the terms and explicitly accept each loan. Repay any amount up to your available cash and outstanding debt, even during a trade between moves. Borrowed money adds the same amount to cash and debt, so it does not increase net worth or your best score.

Each settled market move—including the first—adds 2% interest to outstanding debt, rounded up to the next cent. Interest compounds: a $100 debt becomes $102, then $104.04 after two moves. Selling, borrowing, repayments, and time away do not add interest. At $1,000 debt or more, the loan defaults and the run ends. Borrowing cannot itself reach the limit. Check the next interest charge and repay before a move would cause default.

How do protections work?

Buy one protection between trades in Office. Stop loss costs $35 and recovers 80% of the original invested stake on a crash; Market insurance costs $90 and recovers 100%. Neither covers unsold profit or cancels debt interest. One charge lasts through successful moves and sales until consumed by a crash. Only one protection can be held at a time.

Why does the ticker dip during a winning move?

The short rallies and pullbacks make the ticker feel alive. They are visual fluctuations within a move. The settled outcome is the exact displayed gain or a complete crash, with the odds shown before your decision. Its outcome is fixed when you tap Invest or Hold; closing the app does not reroll it.

What do streaks and desk upgrades do?

Consecutive profitable trades add 8% to the streak component of the reward formula, up to five trades. The dual-monitor desk makes positive moves 15% larger. The offered gain already includes the streak and desk bonuses, plus the increasing reward for holding longer. Crash odds rise with prolonged exposure within a trade. Amber charts and the plant are purely visual. Purchases reset with a new run.

Where is my progress saved?

Your run, debt, pending move, personal best, and preferences are saved on this iPhone. Updating to build 2 keeps your progress and existing protections. An unfinished build 1 move retains its original outcome; every new move follows the rise-or-crash rules. They persist across launches. There is no account or developer-operated cloud sync. We cannot recover progress that was never sent to us.

Why can’t I hear the game?

Greedline respects Silent Mode. Check Silent Mode, media volume, and Sound in the in-game settings. Haptics require supported hardware and can be turned off separately. Reduce effects and iOS Reduce Motion shorten ticker animations.

Is this real investing?

No. Greedline is a fictional game, not an investment service or a simulation of real market behavior. There are no real stocks, real-money trades, ads, or in-app payments.

Which devices are supported?

Greedline is made for iPhone with iOS 17 or later. The app is configured as an iPhone app, with a portrait layout.
