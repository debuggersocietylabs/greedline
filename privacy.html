The app

Greedline | Stock Market Game does not collect or transmit personal information or gameplay data to Debugger Society. The app works offline and has no account system, analytics, tracking, advertising SDKs, or third-party services.

Your current run, cash, debt, purchases made with fictional game cash, personal best, and preferences are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to us or synced through a service operated by us. Deleting the app removes its local data; your device's backup and restore settings are controlled by Apple and you.

Advertising and purchases

Greedline does not display ads. All shop purchases use fictional game cash. There are no real-money purchases, subscriptions, or payment processing.

Support messages

If you choose to email support, we receive your email address and any information you include, such as your message, device details, or screenshots. We use that information to answer your request and troubleshoot the issue.

This website

This website does not include analytics, advertising scripts, forms, or its own tracking cookies.
